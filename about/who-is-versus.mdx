## Our Mission

Versus is building the world's first social prediction network. We're transforming solitary forecasting into a shared, competitive social experience where your track record becomes your reputation.

We believe prediction markets shouldn't be isolated gambling—they should be **social competition**. That's why we're creating a platform where you can:

- Challenge friends directly in 1v1 prediction battles
- Build a verifiable track record that proves your expertise
- Climb leaderboards and earn recognition for your insights
- Connect with a community of skilled forecasters

Our goal is to make prediction markets **fun, social, and skill-based**.

## Why We're Building Versus

We're building Versus for people who want to prove they know best—not just place bets in isolation.

Traditional prediction markets are **solitary and transactional**. You make a prediction, wait for the outcome, and move on. There's no community, no competition, no reputation.

Versus changes that by making prediction **social, competitive, and identity-driven**.

We wanted to build a platform that makes predictions:

- **More engaging** through direct competition
- **More social** through friends and leaderboards  
- **More meaningful** through verifiable track records

Our vision is a world where your prediction skill is recognized, your insights are valued, and every forecast is an opportunity to prove yourself.
